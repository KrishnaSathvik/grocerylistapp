Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Grocery List works without sign-up. Your lists stay on your device.

How do I share a list? Open your list, tap Share, and send a QR code or text. The other person imports their own copy.

Is my data private? Yes. Lists are stored locally unless you choose to share or back them up. Read our privacy policy for details.

Response time

We read every message. Most support requests receive a reply within a few business days.
