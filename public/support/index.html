Email support

Send feedback from the app

Open More → Send Feedback to compose an email. The draft may include basic diagnostics such as the app version, iOS version, and device model to help us troubleshoot. You can review and edit the email before sending it. Grocery-list contents are not included automatically.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Groceries works without sign-up. Your lists are stored locally on your device.

How do I share a list? Open the list and tap the share icon in the header. Use Send Link or Show QR Code so someone can import their own editable copy. Use Copy as Text when you only want to send a readable checklist.

How do I import a list? Go to More → Import a List. Scan a shared-list QR code or paste a Groceries shared-list link.

Can a plain-text checklist be imported? No. A list shared with Copy as Text is only a readable checklist. To create an importable copy, the sender must use Send Link or Show QR Code.

Is my data private? Yes. Your everyday grocery data stays on your device unless you intentionally create a share link or QR code. Read our privacy policy for details.

Response time
