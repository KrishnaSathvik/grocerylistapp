Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Introduction

Grocery List (“the app”) is designed to help you plan and manage grocery shopping with as little friction as possible. This policy explains what information the app uses, how your data is stored, and what happens when you share a list.

We believe grocery planning should be simple and private. No account is required to create and use lists in the app.

Information we collect

The app does not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or contact details simply because you use the app.

Grocery list data — including list names, items, quantities, stores, categories, and preferences — is stored locally on your device.

If you choose to send feedback by email, we receive only the information you include in your message. Optional diagnostics (such as app version, iOS version, and device model) are sent only when you choose to send feedback. Grocery list contents are not included by default.

How the app works

Grocery List is a local-first app. Your lists and settings are primarily stored on your iPhone using on-device storage. The app is designed to work without an internet connection for everyday list management.

The current version of the app does not include advertising or third-party tracking for analytics or marketing purposes.

Shared lists

Lists are only shared when you choose to share them — for example, by generating a QR code or shared text. The exported data contains the list information you choose to export.

When someone imports a shared list, they receive their own copy on their device. Sharing is user-initiated and under your control.

Data storage

Your grocery data remains on your device. The app does not sync your lists to our servers.

If you use device backup services (such as iCloud Backup), your app data may be included in those backups according to Apple’s policies and your device settings.

Third-party services

The app may use standard Apple platform services (such as the App Store, system share sheet, and email) when you choose to use those features.

Apple and the App Store may collect information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review Apple’s privacy documentation for details about platform-level data handling.

The Grocery List website (including this privacy page) may be hosted on a third-party platform. Basic server logs from the hosting provider may include standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

Children’s privacy

Grocery List is a general-purpose utility app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
